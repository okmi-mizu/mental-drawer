# Mental Drawer

A workshop of half-finished thoughts, kept in good order.

Welcome to Mental Drawer, where creativity meets Victorian-era machinery — brass fittings,
patient gearwork, and the conviction that a thing worth building is worth building slowly.

## About This Site

Mental Drawer is a small, deliberately unhurried collection of writing and projects. A few
things you can adjust to taste:

- **Three palettes** — Brass, Copper and Verdigris, each drawn from a different stage of the
  same weathering metal
- **Light and dark** — pick one, or let *Auto* follow your system
- **A collapsible workbench** — fold the navigation away and the page widens to meet you
- **A scroll gear** — the gear in the corner turns in proportion to how far down a page you
  are, and returns you to the top when clicked

All of it lives under [Settings](settings.md).

## The Workshop

Every great invention starts with a solid foundation. Here in the workshop we gather our tools,
organise our thoughts, and prepare for the work ahead.

### The Foundation

This is the calm before creation — clear skies and limitless possibilities, deep waters of
knowledge, and a bench swept clean enough to begin.

### Getting Started

There is no particular order to any of this. [Depth](Depth.md) holds the longer thoughts,
[Videos](videos.md) the moving ones, and [Merch](merch.md) the ones you can wear.

## The Forge

Scroll deeper and you reach the forge — where raw materials are transformed through heat and
pressure. Ideas born in the workshop are tested here, and most of them do not survive.

### Transformation Through Fire

The forge represents the intensity of focused work, the heat of transformation, and the depth
of dedication the work demands.

### The Process

Like metal being shaped on an anvil, the work here asks for:

1. **Patience** — good things take time
2. **Precision** — every detail matters
3. **Persistence** — never give up on your vision

---

## The Illumination

Past the forge the light comes up, and this is where ideas come to life — where prototypes
become real objects with weight to them.

### The Light of Discovery

Breakthrough moments, creative inspiration, and the plain joy of having made a thing that
did not exist yesterday.

### Sharing Knowledge

In this illuminated space we share what we've learned: tutorials and guides, documentation
and references, and the tips that only come from getting it wrong first.

---

## The Archives

Finally, the archives — a quiet place where completed works are stored and, occasionally,
admired.

### Preservation and Reflection

Wisdom gained through experience, the majesty of completed works, and the mystery of whatever
comes next.

### Looking Forward

Even as we file away what's finished, the next thing is already on the bench. The gears never
stop turning.
