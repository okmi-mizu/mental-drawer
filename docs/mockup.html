⚙️ Interactive Features

🔄 Rotating Gears

Four gears on the left side rotate as you scroll, alternating directions for mechanical authenticity

🎨 Dynamic Backgrounds

Background smoothly transitions through 4 themes as you scroll:

  • Blue (0-25%)
  • Brown (25-50%)
  • Yellow (50-75%)
  • Purple (75-100%)

💧 Water Drips

Animated water droplets fall from the "Merch" pipe into the donation bucket below, creating a visual connection

💨 Steam Effects

Steam rises from a crack near the "Videos" link, suggesting fresh, hot content being produced

🚂 Loading Train

A steampunk train slides in from the right when pages are loading, complete with chugging animation and smoke puffs

🔗 Pipe Navigation

Three clickable pipe-styled buttons:

🎥 Videos 🛍️ Merch 💰 Donations

🚀 How to View

To see your steampunk site in action:

  1. Navigate to the /Users/jason/wwd/mental-drawer/docs directory
  2. Start a local server (e.g., python -m http.server 3000)
  3. Open your browser to http://localhost:3000
  4. Scroll down to see the theme changes and rotating gears!
